# Team deployment

Commanders assign responders to zones and move them during an incident. Responders see only their own assignment; administrators can view.

## Assigning and moving responders

- **Current assignments** lists who is where. Use **Move to zone** to reassign a responder; the live map updates for everyone straight away.
- Add a responder under **Assign a responder**, choosing the person and the zone.
- **Release** removes someone from the incident.

## History

**Assignment history** keeps every assignment with its start and end time. It also appears in the incident report.

## For responders

You see the incident and zone you are assigned to. Open **Zone capture** to stream that zone; it is listed first there.
