# Zone capture

Stream your phone camera so the system can classify a zone's structural safety. Made for a phone held in one hand.

## Before you start

- The page must be opened over **HTTPS** (the address starts with `https://`).
- Allow camera access when the browser asks. If you refused, open the browser's site settings and allow the camera, then reload.
- Close other apps that use the camera.

## Capturing a zone

1. Choose the incident and the zone. Zones assigned to you are listed first.
2. Press the big **Start capture** button and point the camera at walls, columns, ceiling, floor and doorways.
3. The large status chip shows the zone's current status with colour, icon and word. The overlay marks detected damage.
4. Critical changes are spoken aloud. Keep the screen on; the app asks the phone not to sleep while capturing.
5. Press **Stop capture** when you leave the zone.

## When the image cannot be analysed

Heavy smoke, darkness or a covered lens give **Not analysable**. The zone keeps its previous status; it never turns "safe" because of a bad image. Move closer, wipe the lens or use a light.

## If the connection drops

The screen shows **Connection lost — reconnecting…** and the app reconnects by itself. If the stream stops for good, the commander receives a camera-lost alert; press **Start capture** again when you are back online.
