# Simulation trainer

Practise classifying zones and postures on incident media, then compare your answers with the correct answers and with the model.

## Doing a scenario

1. Choose a scenario card and press **Start**.
2. Each item shows an image or a video. Select each numbered **region** or **person**, on the image or in the list, then pick a label with the large buttons:
   - zones: Safe, Caution, Unsafe, Blocked
   - people: Standing, Sitting, Lying, Bending
3. For videos, use play/pause; **Labelled frame** jumps back to the frame the answers refer to.
4. Move between items with **Next** and **Back**. **Submit answers** is enabled once every target has a label.

## Your result

- **Score** is measured against the correct answers.
- **Agreement with the model** shows how often you chose the same label as the model: useful, but the model can be wrong.
- Switch between **Your answer**, **Correct answer** and **Model** on each item. Read **Why** for the explanation and **What the model saw** for the model's reasons.
- If enabled, AI instructor feedback explains your mistakes. It never changes the score.
- Press **Try again** to retry; every attempt is kept.

Scenarios marked **PLACEHOLDER** use generated images until real training media is available.

## For training staff and commanders

**Trainee results** lists every attempt. Filter by trainee or scenario, compare trainees, and select a trainee to see their progress chart. Open any attempt to see their answers.
