# Incident reports

Every closed incident has a report. Commanders and administrators can open them.

## Finding a report

Filter by status, search by code, address or city, and limit by date. Select an incident code to open its report. Open incidents link to live monitoring instead.

## What the report contains

- A written summary in English and Urdu.
- Key figures: zones, responders, people found and rescued, critical alerts, the highest zone risk and the duration.
- Each zone's final and highest status and a chart of how zone risk changed over time.
- People detected, team assignments (including reassignments), alerts and a timeline.
- Representative frames and the version of every model that was used.

## PDF

Use **PDF (English)** or **PDF (اردو)** to download a printable A4 report. Times are printed in Pakistan time. Every page carries the decision-support disclaimer.

## AI-written summary

If your administrator has enabled it, the summary may be rewritten by AI from the recorded facts only; it is marked as such. Without it, a standard summary is always written.

## Regenerate and delete

Commanders can **Regenerate** a report (for example after marking more people rescued). Administrators can **Delete** an incident after confirming; the deletion is recorded in the audit log.
