# Settings

Your personal preferences on this device.

- **Language:** English or اردو. The whole interface, including help and spoken alerts, follows it.
- **Spoken alerts:** read critical alerts aloud in your language. The voice comes from your device; Urdu voices are not available on every device, and English is used when none is installed.
- **Sound on critical alerts:** a short sound when a critical alert arrives.
- **Getting-started tour → Replay tour:** show the walkthrough again.
- **Change password:** at least 10 characters with letters and numbers.

System-wide settings (thresholds, AI features) are under **Administration** and only administrators can change them.
