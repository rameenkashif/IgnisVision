# Thermal analysis

Upload thermal images or videos to detect people, their posture and their rescue priority.

## Uploading

1. Choose the incident and, if you know it, the zone the footage is from.
2. Drag a file onto the drop area or choose one: JPEG/PNG images up to 20 MB, MP4/WebM/MOV videos up to 300 MB.
3. The upload shows its progress, then the analysis job runs. You can leave the page; the results are kept.

## Reading the results

- Each person gets a box with their **posture** (standing, sitting, lying, bending) and a **priority** chip: Critical, High, Medium or Low.
- **⚠ low confidence** means the posture is uncertain; check the person yourself.
- Videos are analysed at two frames per second. Use play/pause and the slider to step through the analysed frames; the boxes match each frame exactly. The original file can still be downloaded.

## How priority is worked out

The Rescue Priority Index combines posture (lying is most urgent), detection confidence and the risk of the zone the person is in. If the zone's status is unknown, the other two parts are used. People entering the critical band raise a critical alert on the dashboard.

Detections are decision support. A person may be missed behind obstacles or in heavy heat; always search.
