# Getting started

Ignis Vision helps Rescue 1122 teams assess zone safety and find people inside a building during a structural fire or collapse. **It is decision support only:** the commander decides, and a missing detection never means nobody is there.

## Signing in

1. Enter the username and password from your administrator.
2. The first time, choose **English** or **اردو**. You can switch at any time with the language buttons at the top.
3. A short walkthrough explains your screens. You can replay it from **Settings → Getting-started tour → Replay tour**.

If you were given a temporary password you must choose a new one (at least 10 characters, with letters and numbers).

## What you see depends on your role

- **Incident Commander:** live monitoring, dispatch, team deployment, zone capture, thermal analysis, reports, training.
- **Field Responder:** zone capture with the phone camera, thermal analysis, your own assignment, training.
- **Administrator:** users, training scenarios, system settings, system status and the audit log; live screens are read-only.
- **Training Staff:** the simulator and every trainee's results.
- **Trainee:** the simulator and your own results.

## The top bar on every screen

- **Language:** English / اردو. Switching keeps you on the same screen.
- **Connection indicator:** green when the server and live updates are reachable. If it turns red, data on screen may be out of date; the last update time is shown.
- **Camera indicator:** appears on screens that use the camera.
- **?** opens the help page for the screen you are on.

## Signing out

Use **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu. You are signed out automatically after 30 minutes without activity, except while an active incident is on screen.
